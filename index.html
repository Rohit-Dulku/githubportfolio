Engineering Projects

Lighted Wall Panel (Capstone)

Altium Power Systems

Developed a 48-cell interactive panel for physical therapy. I transitioned the architecture to 12V and designed a custom Arduino shield to manage 576 LEDs efficiently.

Enhanced motor skills and engagement through lighting and audio feedback.

System Control: Managed by multiple Arduino Mega boards

LED Cells: Custom 3D-printed enclosures.

User Input: Controlled by rotary encoders.

For the mechanical design

3D-Printed Enclosures: Custom designed to house 12 LEDs and rotary encoder to ensure durability.

Material Choice: PLA used for the enclosure and PETG used for transparent cover.

Tactile Feedback: Rotary encoder and custom knob cover offer tactility during rotation

For the circuit and electrical design

Schematic Layout: Eight LED cells per Arduino Mega and allows for modulelarity and independent control

Custom Arduino Shield: Streamlines connections and reduces wiring complexity.

Power management: Upgraded from 5V to 12V architecture to reduce current draw and improve efficiency for 576 LEDs. Also decoupling capacitors added for power stability.

For the software portion

LED Control: Code manages individual LED cells for brightness and color modes.

Encoder Input Handling: Encoders required pullup resistors to ensure accurate input.

Modular Code Structure: Each cell operates independently, allowing for scalability
